Player support

Let’s keep your collection safe.

For gameplay, progress, account, or accessibility help, contact Nestarium Support.

Email support

support@playnestarium.com

Include your device, browser or app version, what happened, and what you expected. Redact personal information from screenshots. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, authentication tokens, payment details, or a full save unless support specifically requests it.

Before troubleshooting a save

Do not clear browser data, uninstall, remove a player, or import a different save while investigating missing progress. Those actions can remove the only usable local copy.

Where is my progress saved?

Nestarium saves progress on your device. Supported private-test builds can also synchronize core progress to a cloud copy associated with that installation. A guest cloud identity is not necessarily recoverable after the browser or installation is lost.

How do I back up or transfer a save?

Open Settings → Account & Saves and use Export Save. Keep the file private. Import replaces saved data at the destination, so export that device first. Exports do not transfer authentication credentials.

How do I report a problem?

Describe the screen and action involved, whether it repeats, and any visible error. A redacted screenshot can help.

How do I request or remove cloud data?

Removing a local player does not automatically delete cloud records. See account and data requests before removing local information that may help identify the account.
