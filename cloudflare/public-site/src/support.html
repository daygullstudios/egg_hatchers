Email support

support@playnestarium.com

Include your device, browser or app version, what happened, and what you expected. Redact personal information from screenshots. Never send passwords, sign-in codes, authentication tokens or payment details.

Before troubleshooting a save

Do not clear browser data, uninstall, delete a player, or import a different save while investigating missing progress. Those actions can remove the only usable copy.

Where is my progress saved?

Game progress is saved on your device. In supported private-test builds, an anonymous Firebase identity can also sync core progress. An anonymous cloud copy is not a sign-in account you can recover on a fresh browser or installation.

Google linking and cross-device account recovery are not enabled in the current playtest. Custom artwork, custom eggs and device settings are not all included in core cloud sync.

How do I back up or transfer a save?

Open Settings → Account & Saves and use Export Save. Keep the file private: it includes local player information and settings. Import replaces the destination’s saved data, so export that device first. Exports do not transfer authentication credentials.

Changing the website address creates a separate browser storage area. An existing save does not automatically follow to a new address.

How do I report a problem?

Describe the screen and action involved, whether it repeats, and any visible error. Send a redacted screenshot if helpful. Do not attach a complete save unless support requests it and you choose to share it.

How do I remove an account or request my data?

The in-app Remove local player control removes the local player and saved progress; it does not delete Firebase identity or cloud records. See account and data requests before removing your local player.

Privacy and legal requests can also go to legal@daygullstudios.com.
