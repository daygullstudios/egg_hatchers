Accounts & data

Removing a player
is not the same as
deleting cloud data.

The current private-test Delete Account control removes a player’s local progress. It does not delete Firebase Authentication or Firestore records.

Contact support before clearing your browser, uninstalling or removing a local player if you want help identifying its cloud data. An anonymous identity may be impossible to identify from a name alone after the device data is gone.

Request access, correction or deletion

  1. Email support@playnestarium.com with the subject “Nestarium account data request”.
  2. Describe the request, your chosen player name, the device or browser used, and approximately when you played. Avoid unnecessary personal information.
  3. Wait for the verification instructions. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, authentication tokens, government ID or a full save file in the initial request.

We verify ownership before disclosing or deleting data. A display name alone is not proof. Deletion is not performed merely because an email asks for it. We will explain the verified scope, any applicable retention, and whether more steps are needed for local copies or support correspondence.

Current limitations

Self-service cloud-account deletion and recovery are not yet available in this test. Removing local progress is irreversible without a suitable backup and does not cancel or erase an unrelated Google account.

Privacy questions can also go to legal@daygullstudios.com.
