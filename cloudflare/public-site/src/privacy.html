Privacy · September 6, 2026 draft

Your collection.
Your information.

Nestarium is an idle animal-collection and battle game from Daygull Studios LLC. This draft describes the current private test, not unlaunched sign-in or store features.

Local game data

The game saves player names and usernames, avatar choices, animals, currencies, quests, upgrades, battle progress, custom eggs and artwork, settings, and save backups on your device. A save export contains local player information and settings. Treat it as private.

Cloud progress and authentication

Supported builds use Firebase Authentication to establish an anonymous identity and Firebase Firestore to synchronize core progress. Cloud records include that account identifier, game progress, save revisions, timestamps and an integrity fingerprint. Anonymous does not mean no data is processed. Device settings and custom content are not all included in the core cloud save.

Google account linking and cross-device recovery are not enabled in the current playtest. Before enabling them, we will update these disclosures and the relevant in-app controls. The game currently does not request access to Gmail messages or Google contacts.

Online gameplay

When you join an available online test, the game exchanges player identifiers, chosen names and avatars, presence, team or collection information, battle state, trade offers and preset messages with the session service and relevant players. Do not put personal contact details in a player name. Custom artwork is device-local in the current implementation and is not shown to other players.

Website, access and support

Cloudflare hosts and protects our sites. Technical requests can include IP addresses, browser details and security logs. Protected playtest access may process a sign-in email and session cookies; that access login is separate from your game account. Public information pages do not require a game account.

If you email support, Google and Cloudflare help deliver and store your address, message and any attachments so Daygull Studios can respond. Please send only what is necessary to explain the issue.

How information is used and shared

We use information to run the game, save progress, provide requested online interactions, respond to support, and protect service integrity. Google Firebase and Cloudflare process information as service providers. Relevant gameplay information is shared with other players as described above. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect people and the service.

The current game contains no advertising, purchase, Analytics or Crashlytics SDK. This does not exclude technical logs maintained by hosting and authentication providers. Future data collection or monetization will require updated disclosures and applicable controls before release.

Retention, security and requests

Local data persists until removed through the game, device or browser, subject to browser storage eviction. Cloud copies and support messages are separate from local data. Removing a local player or uninstalling does not itself erase Firebase records or support correspondence.

Request access, correction or deletion using account and data requests. We need to verify that you are entitled to the requested data before acting. Any retention needed for security or legal obligations will be explained as applicable. Service connections use encryption and access controls; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children and audience

AUDIENCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED: The intended audience and any required child-privacy controls must be reviewed before this policy becomes effective. This draft does not assert that Nestarium is child-directed or that it excludes under-13 players.

Contact and changes

For privacy requests, contact legal@daygullstudios.com. For game help, contact support@playnestarium.com. An effective version and material-change notice will accompany the approved policy.
