Nestarium is an idle animal-collection and battle game from Daygull Studios LLC. This draft describes the current private test, not unlaunched sign-in or store features.

Local game data

The game saves player names and usernames, avatar choices, animals, currencies, quests, upgrades, battle progress, custom eggs and artwork, settings, and save backups on your device. A save export contains local player information and settings. Treat it as private.

Cloud progress and authentication

Supported private-test builds automatically attempt to establish a Firebase Authentication identity and synchronize core progress through Firebase Firestore after local startup checks. Cloud records include that account identifier, game progress, save revisions, timestamps and an integrity fingerprint. Anonymous does not mean no data is processed; authentication also involves technical request data such as IP addresses and browser details. Device settings and custom content are not all included in the core cloud save.

Google account linking and cross-device recovery are not enabled in the current playtest. Before enabling them, we will update these disclosures and the relevant in-app controls. The game currently does not request access to Gmail messages or Google contacts.

Online gameplay

The current protected playtest connects to authenticated online services when a player enters Online Arena or Online Trading. The server receives a Firebase account identifier and manages a separate Online Roster, battle state, ratings, rewards, trade offers and preset messages. Other players see a server-created alias rather than the chosen local player name or username. Custom artwork and the local Hatchery Collection are not shared or used as hosted trade authority.

Battle and trade screens allow preset-only reports and blocks; reports do not accept free text or a player-supplied account identifier. A gameplay-shard recovery record contains the reporter and reported Firebase identifiers, selected reason, battle or trade identifier, and timestamps. A central Cloudflare review record replaces those account identifiers with one-way hashes and also stores the source pool, status and expiry. Central reports are scheduled for deletion after 180 days. A block retains the account identifiers needed to exclude the two accounts from future battle and trade matching. Blocking an active trade cancels it before inventory moves, while blocking during a battle does not change the result. Final moderation review, blocked-player management and verified account-deletion procedures remain release-review gates.

A future public hosted-capability registry is implemented but inactive. It stores a hashed Firebase identifier, policy version, allow or deny decision, separate battle, trading and preset-message permissions, an opaque review reference, revision, expiry and any revocation time. It does not store a birth date, email, player name, guardian identity or proof of consent. No live public capability decision has been issued, and the registry does not itself determine age or establish parental consent.

Website, access and support

Cloudflare hosts and protects our sites. Technical requests can include IP addresses, browser details and security logs. Protected playtest access may process a sign-in email and session cookies; that access login is separate from your game account. Public information pages do not require a game account.

If you email support, Google and Cloudflare help deliver and store your address, message and any attachments so Daygull Studios can respond. Please send only what is necessary to explain the issue.

How information is used and shared

We use information to run the game, save progress, provide requested online interactions, respond to support, and protect service integrity. Google Firebase and Cloudflare process information as service providers. Relevant gameplay information is shared with other players as described above. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect people and the service.

The current game contains no advertising, purchase, Analytics or Crashlytics SDK. This does not exclude technical logs maintained by hosting and authentication providers. Future data collection or monetization will require updated disclosures and applicable controls before release.

Retention, security and requests

Local data persists until removed through the game, device or browser, subject to browser storage eviction. Central moderation reports are scheduled for deletion 180 days after creation; matching shard recovery copies use the same retention window. Blocks remain while needed to prevent the blocked accounts from being matched. Other cloud copies and support messages are separate from local data. Removing a local player or uninstalling does not itself erase Firebase records, hosted game records or support correspondence.

Request access, correction or deletion using account and data requests. We need to verify that you are entitled to the requested data before acting. Any retention needed for security or legal obligations will be explained as applicable. Service connections use encryption and access controls; no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children and audience

Our intended audience includes ages 8–12 alongside teens and adults. That product direction is confirmed; the legal classification and required protections are not yet approved.

AUDIENCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED: The current private test does not yet implement an age-assessment or parent-permission boundary before identity, cloud sync or a player’s requested online participation. Parent-managed accounts, verifiable parental consent, revocation and cloud-deletion controls are not yet implemented. This is not a claim of child-privacy compliance or a policy ready for public family release. The final eligibility, data practices, parental notices and controls require review and implementation before publication.

Contact and changes

For privacy requests, contact legal@daygullstudios.com. For game help, contact support@playnestarium.com. An effective version and material-change notice will accompany the approved policy.
